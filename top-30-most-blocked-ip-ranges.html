Top 30 Most Blocked IP Ranges Observed in Public Blacklists

Aggregated, statistical overview of IP ranges that most frequently appear in public blacklist datasets. This page reports patterns, not individual IP addresses.
Last updated: January 2026

At-a-Glance Highlights

  • Cloud and hosting infrastructure shows up on blacklists way more than anything else.
  • Spam, proxy abuse, and automated traffic are the usual suspects.
  • We grouped the data by IP range and ASN, so you won't find individual IPs here.

How This Data Was Compiled

We pulled this dataset from multiple public IP reputation and DNS blacklists. Then we grouped everything by IP range and ASN to spot patterns in how often different providers and regions show up on these lists.

The frequency numbers are kept simple-just High, Medium, or Low-so you're not reading too much into raw counts. It's a more honest way to use the data.
